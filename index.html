The Problem

Young people in Nairobi’s high-density neighbourhoods (such as Kibera, Mathare, Pipeline, and Githurai) encounter uncollected waste daily but cannot sort or dispose of it correctly because waste sorting guidance, recycling drop-off locations, and clean-up event information are scattered across adult-facing websites, NGO pages, and WhatsApp groups that no single student knows how to navigate.

SortRight solves this by providing instant, student-friendly sorting guidance tailored for Nairobi residents — helping you know exactly where every item belongs.

Why This Matters

Nairobi generates over 3,000 tonnes of waste daily, but only about 40% is formally collected, leaving uncollected plastic waste to block vital urban drainage systems across local neighbourhoods.


"I know plastic is bad for the environment, but when I tried to find a recycling bin near me, I just ended up on a bunch of NGO Facebook pages with outdated PDF maps that I couldn't read on my phone."
— Brian, 16, Kibera
